# 🌱 AgriSmart

### AI-Powered Smart Agriculture Platform for Plant Health Monitoring and Intelligent Irrigation

AgriSmart is a team project focused on applying **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and smart agriculture concepts** to improve plant health monitoring and agricultural decision-making.

The project explores how plant health analysis and environmental monitoring can be combined with intelligent irrigation and targeted spraying to support more efficient and sustainable farming.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional agricultural practices often depend on manual monitoring of crops and fixed irrigation or spraying schedules. This can make it difficult to identify plant diseases at an early stage and may lead to unnecessary use of water and agricultural inputs.

AgriSmart explores a technology-driven approach to:

* Monitor plant health
* Identify possible plant diseases using AI-based analysis
* Monitor environmental conditions
* Assess infection levels
* Support intelligent irrigation and targeted spraying decisions

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🌿 Plant Health Monitoring

The system focuses on monitoring plant health and identifying potential disease or infection through plant analysis.

### 🤖 AI-Based Disease Analysis

AI and machine learning concepts are explored for analyzing plant images and identifying possible plant diseases.

### 📊 Agricultural Data & Analytics

Environmental and agricultural information can be used to support data-driven decisions related to plant health and irrigation.

### 💧 Smart Irrigation

The project explores intelligent irrigation based on plant and environmental conditions rather than relying only on fixed schedules.

### 🌱 Targeted Spraying

The system concept considers different spraying responses based on infection severity:

```text
Healthy / No Infection
        ↓
     No Spray

Low Infection
        ↓
   Partial Spray

High Infection
        ↓
    Full Spray
```

### 🔌 IoT & Hardware Integration

The project explores the integration of sensors, microcontrollers, and actuators to connect software-based decisions with physical agricultural systems.
